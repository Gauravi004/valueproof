## Distance Evidence

The P4 pipeline calculates distances between the property and available nearby location records.

Example:

- Nearest school: approximately 0.53 km
- Main road: approximately 1.02 km

## How It Helps ValueMap AI

The location layer provides contextual evidence about the property's surroundings.

The information can help explain factors such as:

- accessibility
- connectivity
- nearby services
- buyer/seller confidence

## Important Limitation

The current prototype combines OpenStreetMap data with clearly labelled demo data.

Therefore, this output is location intelligence for the prototype and should not be presented as an official government valuation or official property record.

Location signals are contextual evidence and are not automatically converted into rupee price adjustments.
